# Privacy Policy for SORTIFY

**Last updated:** October 7, 2026

**SORTIFY** ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the SORTIFY application.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
SORTIFY is designed with a **privacy-first, 100% offline architecture**:
- **No Personal Data Collection**: SORTIFY does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, file names, file paths, or directory metadata.
- **No Analytics or Telemetry**: SORTIFY contains zero tracking, analytics, crash reporting, or user monitoring code.
- **No Internet Required**: SORTIFY operates entirely locally on your Windows device and does not require an active internet connection to organize files or detect duplicates.

## 2. Local Filesystem Access
To perform file organization, triage, and duplicate detection:
- SORTIFY accesses only the local directories and files that you explicitly select.
- All file move operations, dry-run previews, SHA-256 chunk hashing, and history logging occur strictly on your local disk (`%USERPROFILE%\.smart_file_organizer`).
- SORTIFY never uploads, shares, or transfers your files or data to any external server.

## 3. Third-Party Services
SORTIFY does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking services, or external APIs.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly in this document on our official GitHub repository.
